Limitations:
Uses global RNFL only — sector and GCC analysis not included. Segmentation errors and scan artefacts may produce apparent thinning. Floor effect limits sensitivity at advanced stages. Short series may show amplified slope variance.

Evidence-informed structural progression analysis based on serial OCT RNFL data. Does not replace clinical judgment or comprehensive evaluation of OCT findings.

This tool analyses longitudinal peripapillary RNFL thickness values using linear regression to estimate the rate of structural change over time. Classification is based on deterministic rule-based logic and is intended as a structured decision support aid, not as a standalone diagnostic conclusion.

OCT RNFL interpretation may be affected by segmentation errors, signal quality artefacts, device variability, anatomic variation (high myopia, tilted disc, peripapillary atrophy, ERM), floor effect, and the inherent limitations of serial trend analysis with short follow-up. RNFL slope estimation requires adequate serial data; results from small or short series should be interpreted with caution. Global RNFL does not capture sector-level or focal structural loss.

The clinician retains full responsibility for all diagnostic and therapeutic decisions. This tool is intended as a decision-support aid, consistent with current glaucoma guidelines, and is not a substitute for clinical evaluation. Not intended for patient self-use.
