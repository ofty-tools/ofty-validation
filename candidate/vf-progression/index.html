Limitations:
Uses global MD only — focal progression may not be captured. No learning effect correction. No pointwise analysis. Short series may show amplified slope variance.

Evidence-informed functional progression analysis based on serial visual field data. Does not replace clinical judgment or comprehensive perimetric evaluation.

This tool analyses longitudinal visual field Mean Deviation values using linear regression to estimate the rate of functional change over time. Classification is based on deterministic rule-based logic and is intended as a structured decision support aid, not as a standalone diagnostic conclusion.

Visual field interpretation may be affected by test reliability, short-term fluctuation, media opacity, cognitive state, disease stage, and instrument variability. MD slope estimation requires adequate serial data; results from small or short series should be interpreted with caution. Global MD does not capture focal progression.

The clinician retains full responsibility for all diagnostic and therapeutic decisions. This tool is intended as a decision-support aid, consistent with current glaucoma guidelines, and is not a substitute for clinical evaluation. Not intended for patient self-use.
